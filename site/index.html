Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes, for founding coaches while we're in this early phase. If that ever changes, we'll tell you well in advance, and you can leave any time.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. Clients pay your own UPI account directly, and the money reaches your bank as soon as they pay. Thela doesn't touch it.

What can I sell?

Monthly coaching plans, 1:1 calls, batches, recorded classes, and guides like workout or diet plans. Not physical products for now, and nothing that promises guaranteed results.

Do my clients need an app or account?

No. They open your link, tap a price and pay with whichever UPI app they already have.

How fast can my store go live?

Usually within 3 working days of getting your details. You approve the store before anyone sees it.

Can I see a store first?

Yes. Open the sample store on your phone and try an order. Nothing real is charged.
